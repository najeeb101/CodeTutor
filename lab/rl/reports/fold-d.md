# GRPO on fold d: before and after

Fold d holds out modules 4, 8: the **held-out** rows are problems the training never used. Both runs went through the same eval pipeline (all 33 problems, 10 answers each, temperature 0.8, plain sampling, graded in Docker). Generated by `scripts/rl_after.py report`.

## 6. What this can and cannot say

- **One fold, one seed, one run.** This report covers fold d alone. All folds and the second seed are compared in folds.md; no single fold carries a conclusion.
- **9 held-out problems.** One problem is a swing of 11 points of pass@1; read the intervals, not the point values.
- **Training used 19 problems** (those whose baseline group was mixed), each seen many times: the train rows show how much it was memorised.
- **Held-out means a different topic, not an unrelated skill** (docs/rl.md §2).
- **A LoRA adapter** can learn less than full fine-tuning. The sampling seed is fixed, so a rerun gives the same answers, but a different seed could move the numbers.
- **The hard-coding check is crude** and cannot see a trick that does not write the answer out.
