# Fold c: two training seeds

The same recipe (LoRA, 100 steps x 4 prompts, partial reward) trained twice, with `--seed 0` and `--seed 1`. Each adapter was evaluated in 3 sampling draws, pooled to 30 answers per problem, like the untrained model. Generated by `scripts/rl_after.py seeds`.

## 1. Do the two seeds agree?

Rule (written before the second run): the seeds **agree** if both changes point the same way and the two changes differ by less than the evaluation band `1.96 x SD / sqrt(m)`; otherwise they **disagree**, and one training run cannot be trusted on its own.

| Problems | Count | pass@1 before | Change seed 0 | Change seed 1 | Seed 1 − seed 0 | 95% interval | Noise band | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| fold c held out | 8 | 0.446 | +0.03 | +0.04 | +0.02 | [−0.02, +0.05] | ±0.064 | seeds agree |
| fold c train | 25 | 0.717 | +0.07 | +0.08 | +0.01 | [−0.01, +0.03] | ±0.036 | seeds agree |

## 2. Limits

- SD used: 0.092 (untrained model, 3 draws). Two seeds are the minimum: the difference between them is itself one noisy number.
- The interval resamples problems only. Agreement of two seeds does not prove transfer: it only says the result is repeatable. Whether a change is above noise is decided by `pooled.md`.
