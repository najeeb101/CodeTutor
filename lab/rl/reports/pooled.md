## 3. Limits

- SD used: 0.092 for one problem's before -> after difference (`sqrt(2 x v / 3)`, `v` from the untrained model's 3 draws, z = 1.96). With 3 draws the SD itself is uncertain.
- This is evaluation noise only. **Training noise** (another training seed) is still unmeasured and can be larger: a fold may be 'above noise' here and still be luck of one training run.
- The interval resamples problems; the band treats problems as independent.
- Held-out means a different topic, not an unrelated skill (docs/rl.md §2). The rule fixes what counts as above noise; it does not prove transfer.
